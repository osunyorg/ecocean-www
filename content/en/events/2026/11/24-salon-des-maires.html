In 2026, the The Biodiversity and Ecological Engineering Expo will establish itself as the must-attend event for stakeholders who preserve, restore, and integrate biodiversity into land-use planning and adaptation.

Against a backdrop of growing challenges related to climate change, biodiversity loss, and the ecological transition, it brings together all stakeholders: local governments, institutions, associations, businesses, land-use planners, engineering firms, natural area managers, and experts in ecological engineering.

Over the course of three days, the fair fosters networking, the sharing of experiences, and the discovery of concrete solutions to support regional adaptation, guided by a strong conviction: nature at the heart of regional adaptation. The Biodiversity and Ecological Engineering Expo is organized jointly with the Sports and Parasports Expo and the Mayors and Local Governments Expo.
